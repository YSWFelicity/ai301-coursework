## Checks

| Check                | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                             | Pass condition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Weight    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |

A smaller or different implementation may pass when the deviation is honestly explained and the PR description does not falsely claim exact plan fidelity. ## Verdict rule

Grade every check as `pass`, `fail`, or `unclear` using the evidence and pass condition in its row.

Return `accept` only when every `required` check passes.

Return `reject` if any `required` check is `fail` or `unclear`. An `unclear` grade means the available evidence is insufficient to verify that check; do not assume missing facts are favorable.

`preferred` checks never change the verdict, regardless of whether they are `pass`, `fail`, or `unclear`.

Always evaluate every check before assembling the final verdict. Do not stop at the first failure.

Apply the honest-outcome rule: a disclosed limitation, smaller-than-planned implementation, or unperformed optional check does not automatically require rejection. Decide whether the actual submitted change, the observable evidence, and the applicable repository requirements satisfy the stated pass conditions. A disclosure does not excuse unrelated changes, false claims, or a missing essential verification.

The only possible final verdicts are `accept` and `reject`. Provide a specific evidence line for every check.
