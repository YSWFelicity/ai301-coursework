# Voice guide: how I talk upstream

## Who I am in threads

I am a student contributor learning to work with an existing codebase.
I am investigating a specific issue and will report what I actually observe.
Readers can expect short, specific updates without promises I cannot verify.

## Rules I write by

### Rule: Say what I will investigate

When claiming an issue, I describe what I plan to check instead of saying that I already know the cause or solution.

- Wrong: "I know what is causing this and I will fix it."
- Right: "I’d like to investigate this issue and check the README and `.env.example` to understand the configuration mismatch."

### Rule: Report only what I observed

I separate what I actually observed from guesses about the cause.

- Wrong: "The API key mismatch definitely causes the application to fail."
- Right: "In my environment, I observed this behavior after following these steps."

### Rule: Do not promise a fix or deadline

I can promise to investigate and report back, but I do not promise that I will fix the issue or finish by a certain date.

- Wrong: "I’ll have this fixed by tomorrow."
- Right: "I’ll investigate this and post a reproduction report with what I find."

### Rule: Keep issue comments focused

I include information that helps someone understand or repeat my investigation and avoid unrelated background.

- Wrong: "I’m new to open source and this is for my class, so I hope I can figure this out."
- Right: "I tested this using the environment and steps below."

### Rule: Be transparent about limitations

If I cannot reproduce the reported behavior, I say so clearly instead of forcing a successful result.

- Wrong: "The issue is reproduced."
- Right: "I could not reproduce the reported behavior in this environment after following the steps below."

## Things I never post

- A promise that I will definitely fix an issue.
- A completion date I cannot guarantee.
- A claim that I reproduced something before I actually tested it.
- A guessed root cause presented as a confirmed fact.
- A reproduction result that is stronger than the evidence I collected.
- Boilerplate that does not say what I actually investigated.
